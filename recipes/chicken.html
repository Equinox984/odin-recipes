Chicken Alfredo Crescent Roll Casserole

Description

This chicken Alfredo crescent roll casserole is a creamy, cheesy comfort food dinner that's easy to put together. Crescent roll dough is stuffed with a chicken-and-spinach filling, then baked with Alfredo sauce and plenty of melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 10 ounces frozen chopped spinach, thawed and drained well
  • 3/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 2 (8-ounce) packages refrigerated crescent rolls, unrolled and separated into triangles
  • 1 (15 ounce) jar Alfredo sauce
  • 1 cup milk
  • 8 ounces shredded Italian cheese blend
  • chopped fresh parsley for garnish
